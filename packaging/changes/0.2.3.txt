Everything in v0.2.2 is in it, plus:

GAME (build c432)
- Sound packs: replace the game's sound effects, the announcer's lines and the music with your own WAV files.
  Build a pack with one command and install it from the launcher's Mods tab. A sound pack follows the Mods tab's
  on/off switch and order. With no sound pack switched on, nothing changes.
- Tools (Python only): "sound_pack.py export" saves the game's own sounds as named WAV files from your game folder or
  disc image; "sound_pack.py import" checks your edited WAVs and builds the pack.

LAUNCHER
- The Mods tab shows "Sound pack" for sound packs (next to "Texture pack").
- The modding guide (docs/MODDING.md) has a new walkthrough, "Replace sounds and music", with what can be replaced,
  the file rules and a clear note: share only audio you made or have the rights to; packs with copyrighted music or the
  game's own voice clips are for personal use only.

KNOWN ISSUES
- Sound packs: making a replaced sound loop (a file name ending in _loop) is untested in the game.
- Sound packs: the first start with a pack takes a few seconds longer while the game prepares the changed sounds.
- Unlimited draw distance can still show buildings popping in and out at the far skyline (not fixed, not verified either way).
- In Tokyo a UFO can leave the playing area (out of bounds). Not fixed yet.
- The announcer's "RAGE" line plays on a rage pickup; tell us if you don't hear it.
- Retexturing: every monster has one skin set (colour and bump map). Menu name plates are not power-of-two pictures and
  may look stretched if you change their shape.
- Typing a name was tested with a virtual keyboard path only, not with a physical keyboard.
- Playing from a disc image was tested with images made from an extracted game folder, plain and with a video
  partition in front. Images dumped from a real disc have not been tested yet.
- A rare crash while loading, seen on slow systems. Still being investigated.
- A possible rare stall in the game's DirectSound audio code, seen in two test runs. Not reproduced, not fixed.
- With the in-game menu open the picture is redrawn only when something changes, so it can look choppy in video.
- Exclusive fullscreen is less tested than the other modes.
- A real physical controller has not been tried with the in-game menu (the tests used a virtual one).
  Only Xbox controllers have been tested. Windows only.

PACKAGE
- Same files as v0.2.2, including ste_overlay.dll (the in-game menu).
