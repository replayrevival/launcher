Everything in v0.1.5a is in it, plus:

GAME (build c408)
- Mechagodzilla 3's back missiles now show their heads and their smoke trails. The same fix repairs other
  particle effects that were drawn wrongly (small sprites that used the wrong picture).
- You can type your profile name with the PC keyboard on the on-screen keyboard (Tab does END).
- For people who make videos: an optional recorder (set STE_RECORD) and a log line for frames that took
  more than a quarter of a second.

LAUNCHER
- Unchanged from v0.1.5a.

KNOWN ISSUES
- Mechagodzilla 2's finger rockets were not checked specifically.
- A rare crash while loading, seen on slow systems. Still being investigated.
- A possible rare stall in the game's DirectSound audio code, seen in two test runs. Not reproduced, not fixed.
- One longer stall in a water stage was seen once in a 4K test run and did not repeat (the game now logs such
  frames, so a report with the log helps).
- On PlayStation style a few short lines say "PRESS MENU" where "PRESS OPTIONS" does not fit.

PACKAGE
- Same files as v0.1.5a, including ste_overlay.dll (the in-game menu).
