Everything in v0.2.3 is in it, plus:

GAME (build c434)
- Solo Destruction: a new SOLO DESTRUCTION row on the main menu. Pick any monster and any city and destroy at your own pace.
  ROUND LENGTH is 1 to 10 minutes, or NO TIME LIMIT. A results screen shows at 0:00. Normal Versus is unchanged.
  It is a mod that is built on your PC from your own game files, so the download contains no game data. It is switched OFF
  until you turn it on in the Mods tab. Based on ANILORAK's Godzilla Ultra research (our own rewrite; nothing of his is included).
- Photo mode: pause a fight and fly a free camera. Change the field of view, roll the camera, hide the HUD and save full-resolution
  PNG screenshots. Open it from the in-game menu (PHOTO MODE), with F8, or by holding Back and Y (switch that on in the Controllers tab).

LAUNCHER
- Mods that change game scripts are now built from your own game files when you press PLAY (nothing is downloaded). The Mods tab
  shows "Needs build" until it is done. Works with an unpacked folder and with a disc image.
- The download now contains a small Python (python.org's embeddable Python 3.15.2, about 14 MB) and the game project's mod tools,
  so nothing has to be installed. The Solo Destruction mod is already in your Mods tab, switched off.
- Controllers tab: a "Photo mode" group (on/off, keys, camera speed, controller shortcut). The in-game menu has a PHOTO MODE button.
- The Mods tab labels gameplay mods "Gameplay mod".

KNOWN ISSUES
- Photo mode works during a fight only (not in menus). The picture of the HUD clock keeps counting while the HUD is shown and snaps
  back on close; the match clock itself is frozen. A live free camera while the match runs is not in this release.
- Photo mode: flying with keyboard and mouse and the in-game menu button were not tested by hand (the tests used scripted input).
- Solo Destruction: Mothra in solo is untested, and a 5-digit score banner is untested. The menu text is English only.
- Sound packs: making a replaced sound loop (a file name ending in _loop) is untested in the game.
- Sound packs: the first start with a pack takes a few seconds longer while the game prepares the changed sounds.
- Unlimited draw distance can still show buildings popping in and out at the far skyline (not fixed, not verified either way).
- In Tokyo a UFO can leave the playing area (out of bounds). Not fixed yet.
- The announcer's "RAGE" line plays on a rage pickup; tell us if you don't hear it.
- Retexturing: every monster has one skin set (colour and bump map). Menu name plates are not power-of-two pictures and
  may look stretched if you change their shape.
- Typing a name was tested with a virtual keyboard path only, not with a physical keyboard.
- Playing from a disc image was tested with images made from an extracted game folder, plain and with a video
  partition in front. Images dumped from a real disc have not been tested yet.
- A rare crash while loading, seen on slow systems. Still being investigated.
- A possible rare stall in the game's DirectSound audio code, seen in two test runs. Not reproduced, not fixed.
- With the in-game menu open the picture is redrawn only when something changes, so it can look choppy in video.
- Exclusive fullscreen is less tested than the other modes.
- A real physical controller has not been tried with the in-game menu (the tests used a virtual one).
  Only Xbox controllers have been tested. Windows only.

PACKAGE
- Same files as v0.2.3 plus tools\python (embedded Python), tools\mods (the mod tools) and bundled_mods\ste.solo-destruction.
