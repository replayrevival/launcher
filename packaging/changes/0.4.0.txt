Everything in v0.2.3 is in it, plus the changes of the unreleased 0.3.x work:

GAME (build c437)
- Biollante is playable: P1, COM, a second human player on a second pad, Versus, Melee, Action mode and Solo Destruction. Her models,
  animations, HUD and sounds come from the retail disc; the mod holds only a recipe and is built on your PC (nothing is downloaded).
  It is switched OFF until you turn it on in the Mods tab. The select screen gets a third row for her.
- Solo Destruction: a SOLO DESTRUCTION row on the main menu. Any monster, any city, ROUND LENGTH 1 to 10 minutes or NO TIME LIMIT,
  a results screen at 0:00. Also a mod built on your PC, OFF until you switch it on. Inspired by ANILORAK's Godzilla Ultra research
  (our own code; none of his code or files are used).
- Photo mode: pause a fight and fly a free camera. Field of view, camera roll, HUD on/off and full-resolution PNG screenshots. Open it
  from the in-game menu (PHOTO MODE), with F8, or by holding Back and Y (switch that on in the Controllers tab).
- Script mods can be combined: Solo Destruction and Biollante work together. If two mods change the same thing, the launcher names both.

LAUNCHER
- Mods that change game scripts are built from your own game files when you press PLAY, all switched-on ones together in one go.
  The Mods tab shows "Needs build" until it is done. Works with an unpacked folder and with a disc image.
- The download contains a small Python (python.org's embeddable Python 3.15.2, about 14 MB) and the game project's mod tools, so nothing
  has to be installed. Solo Destruction and Biollante are already in your Mods tab, switched off.
- Controllers tab: a "Photo mode" group (on/off, keys, camera speed, controller shortcut). The in-game menu has a PHOTO MODE button.
- The Mods tab labels gameplay mods "Gameplay mod". The credit lines on the Enhancements pages now read "Inspired by ... (our own code) -
  see Credits". CREDITS.md has two parts: code and assets used, and research and inspiration (no code used).

KNOWN ISSUES
- Biollante: when the select screen reopens, a monster in the third row is not preselected. Her AI is the stock Easy/Medium/Hard level.
  Her effects were checked with a scripted sample of attacks, not every move.
- Solo Destruction: the title is English only; Mothra in solo and a 5-digit score banner are untested.
- Photo mode works during a fight only. The picture of the HUD clock keeps counting while the HUD is shown (the match clock itself is
  frozen). Flying with keyboard and mouse and the in-game menu button were not tested by hand.
- Unlimited draw distance can still show buildings popping in and out at the far skyline (not fixed, not verified either way).
- In Tokyo a UFO can leave the playing area (out of bounds). Not fixed yet.
- Sound packs: making a replaced sound loop (a file name ending in _loop) is untested in the game.
- Playing from a disc image was tested with images made from an extracted game folder, not with a dump of a real disc.
- A real physical controller has not been tried with the in-game menu (the tests used a virtual one). Only Xbox controllers have been
  tested. Windows only. A rare crash while loading on slow systems is still being investigated.

PACKAGE
- Same files as v0.2.3 plus tools\python (embedded Python), tools\mods (the mod tools) and bundled_mods\ste.solo-destruction and
  bundled_mods\ste.biollante.
