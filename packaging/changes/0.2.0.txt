Everything in v0.1.7 is in it, plus (v0.1.8 was never released, so its changes are in here too):

GAME (build c426)
- The game reads your disc image (.iso) directly and read-only: nothing is unpacked and your image is never
  changed. Your saves and settings are kept next to the launcher, as before.
- The in-game menu works with a controller alone: D-pad or left stick to move, A to choose, B to go back,
  LB and RB to switch between the Video, Enhancements and Controllers pages, Start to close it. Holding a
  direction repeats. The keyboard still works (arrows, Enter, Esc, Page Up and Page Down for the pages).
- Fixed: with true full screen ("Exclusive fullscreen") or with variable refresh rate switched off, the in-game
  menu did not redraw or react and looked frozen. It now works in every window mode.
- "Fullscreen" is now borderless fullscreen: a borderless window that covers the whole screen, without the
  old switch of display mode. The old true full screen is still there as "Exclusive fullscreen (advanced)".
  Existing settings that said Fullscreen now give borderless fullscreen.
- Typing a name with the on-screen keyboard is about three times as fast as in 0.1.7 (still one letter per press).
- At the highest draw-distance quality step (x4) the game's load governor no longer shrinks the far
  skyline below x3 when the game is busy.

LAUNCHER
- Choose your disc image and press Play: the launcher no longer unpacks it into a folder first. The image is
  checked the same way as before (the game program inside it must match a known-good copy).
  Images with a video partition in front (some dumps) are recognised too. An extracted folder still works,
  and is needed for mods that patch the game's files (they are not applied while you play from an image).
- Fixed: disc images with the game partition at one particular offset (a third-generation Xbox layout) were not
  recognised.
- The Video tab's window mode has four choices: Window, Borderless window, Fullscreen (borderless) and
  Exclusive fullscreen (advanced). The resolution list for both fullscreen modes shows the sizes your
  monitor reports.
- The in-game menu shows its three pages as a tab strip and understands the controller's LB and RB.
- Credit: the Enhancements settings that are based on G:STEM's gameplay options are grouped under
  "G:STEM-inspired options", with the note "Based on G:STEM's gameplay options - credit to Gojirex, Shame Devs,
  GR8GODZILLAGOD and the STEM community." (launcher tab and in-game menu).

KNOWN ISSUES
- Unlimited draw distance can still show buildings popping in and out at the far skyline (not fixed yet).
- In Tokyo a UFO can leave the playing area (out of bounds). Not fixed yet.
- The announcer's "RAGE" voice line is missing. Not fixed yet.
- Playing from a disc image was tested with images made from an extracted game folder, plain and with a video
  partition in front. Images dumped from a real disc have not been tested yet.
- A rare crash while loading, seen on slow systems. Still being investigated.
- A possible rare stall in the game's DirectSound audio code, seen in two test runs. Not reproduced, not fixed.
- With the in-game menu open the picture is redrawn only when something changes, so it can look choppy in video.
- Exclusive fullscreen is less tested than the other modes.
- A real physical controller has not been tried with the in-game menu (the tests used a virtual one).
  Only Xbox controllers have been tested. Windows only.

PACKAGE
- Same files as v0.1.7, including ste_overlay.dll (the in-game menu).
